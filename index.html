How to get your Instagram ZIP file

Request your data export from Instagram's settings, wait for Meta to prepare it, then download and drop the ZIP above. Scroll through the screenshots below for a visual walkthrough.

1

Open Settings

In the Instagram app, go to your profile, then open Settings and privacy.

2

Go to "Download your information"

Under "Accounts Center" or "Your activity", find the data download / export option.

3

Select "Some of your information"

Choose "Followers and following" as the data category to include in the export.

4

Choose format and submit

Set the format to JSON, pick a date range, then submit the export request.

5

Wait for the email

Meta emails you once the export is ready — this can take from a few minutes up to 48 hours.

6

Download the ZIP

Open the email or go back to "Download your information" and download the ZIP file.

Once you have the ZIP file, drop it in the box above. It's read entirely in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
