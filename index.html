How to get your Instagram export

Takes 2 minutes to request. Meta usually emails you within an hour (sometimes up to 48h).

  1. Open "Download your information"Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information. Or use the button below.
  2. Choose "Some of your information"Tick only Followers and following. The file stays tiny and arrives faster.
  3. Download to device · All time · JSONThe format must be JSON. HTML exports can't be read.
  4. Wait for Meta's emailThen download the ZIP from the same page.
  5. Drop the ZIP aboveNo need to unzip it. It's read right here in your browser.

Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your file is opened with JavaScript inside this page, and the page is locked so it can't make network requests. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can even use it offline.

How do I see who unfollowed me?

Instagram doesn't include that in exports. But this tool can remember a snapshot of your followers on this device. Upload a fresh export a few weeks later and you'll get exact "New followers" and "Lost followers" lists.

"Recently unfollowed" shows people I unfollowed?

Correct. Instagram's "recently unfollowed" file lists accounts you unfollowed, not people who unfollowed you. Use snapshots (above) for that.

Why don't the numbers match the app exactly?

Exports are a snapshot from the moment Meta created them, and deactivated accounts can still appear in them. Small differences are normal.
